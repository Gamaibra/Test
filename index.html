The shop

A corner of Uganda on Naif Road.

The Pearl of Africa is a Ugandan grocery in Deira, Dubai. You'll know it by its golden-lit façade and the Ugandan coat of arms above the door.

  • Every weekFresh food and fruit from Uganda
  • On the façadeGreen organic vegetables & fruits
  • Same groupSister shop of the Oryx Village restaurant

Uganda

Why our name

Uganda, the Pearl of Africa.

Uganda has long been called the Pearl of Africa. Every week, its fresh food and fruit make the journey to our shop on Naif Road.

“Uganda is truly the Pearl of Africa.”Winston Churchill, My African Journey, 1908
